## Data from other sites (not distributed here)

The scraper downloads data and media when the user runs it; nothing from these
services is in the repository.

* **ScreenScraper** (screenscraper.fr): covers, screenshots, videos and texts of
  the games. The scraper uses the project's developer key and the user's own
  account; the terms of use of the site apply. The `softname` sent is `MiSTerFrontier`.
* **Wikipedia**: descriptions and technical data of the systems
  (`--systems-info`). The texts are licensed CC BY-SA 4.0; whoever shares the
  generated `gamelist.xml` must credit Wikipedia and its authors and keep the
  same license.
* **ArcadeDB** (adb.arcadeitalia.net): data and pictures of the arcade games.
* **libretro thumbnails** (thumbnails.libretro.com): box art and screenshots
  when ScreenScraper has none; each image keeps the rights of its owners.
* **MAME software lists** (mamedev/mame, `hash/*.xml`): titles and groups of
  the console games.
* No-Intro style file names are only read from the user's own game folders.
