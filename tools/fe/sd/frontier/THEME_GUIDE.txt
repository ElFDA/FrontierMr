THEME GUIDE - rebuilding the "City Pop" theme with the theme editor
===================================================================

This guide makes, step by step, a copy of the City Pop theme
(themes/citypop: Japanese pop of the bubble era, after bubblism.jp) with
the theme editor. At the end you have your own theme, with the same look,
that you can change as you like. Italian version: GUIDA_TEMA_ita.txt.

What a theme is made of:
  theme.ini       the list, 16:9 screens (HDMI)
  theme_43.ini    the list, 4:3 screens (CRT 640x240)
  detail.ini      the detail screen (X on the gamepad), 16:9
  detail_43.ini   the detail screen, 4:3
  img/            pictures: background, selection bar, year tag
  fonts/          TTF fonts
  media/systems/  pictures of the main screen (optional)
The .ini files place the elements (texts, list, pictures) on the screen.
The editor writes the .ini files; the pictures are made with a paint
program (or with a script, see STEP 1).

Coordinates are in "base" units: 1280x720 in the 16:9 files, 960x720 in
the 4:3 ones; Frontier scales them to the real screen. On the CRT
(240 lines) 3 units of height are one line: City Pop keeps rows and
panels on multiples of 3.


STEP 0 - WHAT YOU NEED
  - the editor, one of the two (they do the same, offline), from the
    Releases page of FrontierMr (https://github.com/ElFDA/FrontierMr/releases/latest):
    - the program FrontierThemeEditor for Windows, macOS (Apple Silicon) or
      Linux, nothing to install (see docs/THEME_EDITOR.md for the first start);
    - or the web page theme_editor.html, opened with Chrome or Edge with a
      double click (they save straight into the folder; with other browsers
      use "Download .ini" and copy the files yourself)
  The steps name the buttons of the web page; in the program they are:
    Open folder      File > Open folder (or drop the folder on the window)
    Save to folder   File > Save (Ctrl+S)
    Variant          16:9 | 4:3 and the screen menu in the top bar
    Make 4:3         Make 4:3 in the top bar (also in the Variant menu)
    Make detail      Variant > Make detail
    Import .ini      File > Load .ini
    240p preview     240p preview in the top bar
    Delete           the Del key (Edit > Delete layer)
  - the pictures and fonts of City Pop: from tools/fe/sd/frontier/themes/
    or from /media/fat/frontier/themes/ on the MiSTer


STEP 1 - THE FOLDER OF THE THEME
  Make a folder named like the theme, e.g. mycitypop, with:

    mycitypop/
      img/            copy all the files of themes/citypop/img/
      fonts/          copy BebasNeue-Regular.ttf (titles),
                      PTSansNarrow-Bold.ttf (texts), DejaVuSans-Bold.ttf
                      (the stars) and their LICENSE-*.txt from
                      themes/simple/fonts/
      media/systems/  copy the files of themes/citypop/media/systems/

  City Pop itself takes the fonts from ../simple/fonts/, but the editor
  only sees the files inside the folder you open: with the fonts in
  fonts/ you see them in the editor, and the theme works on the MiSTer too.

  The pictures come from a script (tools/fe/themegen/citypop_assets.py,
  Python 3 with Pillow and numpy). To draw them yourself, these are the
  sizes and the positions (colours #RRGGBB):

    img/bg.png 1280x720 (img/bg_43.png 960x720), background of the list
      - fill #F4D5D5; dashed grid #FFE3F9: vertical lines 3 px wide every
        96 px from x=48, horizontal lines 3 px tall every 60 px from y=30,
        dashes of 18 px with gaps of 3 px
      - dots of radius 3 every 12 px: pink #FFA3D9 in x 14-150, y 104-240;
        mint #69D2BA in x 1150-1274, y 86-206 (4:3: x 830-954)
      - header: blue #3C6BB1 slab with corners (26,92) (40,30) (176,4)
        (160,70), mint #69D2BA slab over it (20,88) (34,26) (170,0)
        (154,66), glossy pink bubble 84 px wide at 36,8
      - panels: light blue shadow #7AA1DB moved 6 px right and down,
        paper #FAF2F1, mint border 16 px wide
          list:     26,114 to 616,632 (4:3: to 466,632), border on the left
          picture:  652,114 to 1254,443 (4:3: 494,114 to 934,443), border
                    on the right
      - help bar: mint 40,648 to 1240,699 (4:3: to 920,699), shadow as above
    img/detail.png 1280x720 (img/detail_43.png 960x720), detail screen
      - as bg.png, but the picture panel is on the left, 26,114 to 616,443
        (4:3: to 466,443), border on the left, and the text panel on the
        right, 652,114 to 1254,632 (4:3: 494,114 to 934,632), border on the
        right; mint dots in x 1150-1274, y 86-206 (4:3: x 830-954)
    img/sel.png 574x51 (img/sel_43.png 424x51), selection bar, transparent:
      light blue #7AA1DB rectangle 5,6 to 573,47 (4:3: to 423,47), mint
      rectangle 0,3 to 567,44 (4:3: to 417,44) over it
    img/tag.png 132x60, tag of the year, transparent: light blue polygon
      (6,6) (126,6) (126,58) (20,49), pink #FFA3D9 polygon (12,0) (132,0)
      (132,52) (26,43) over it
    media/systems/<key>.png 640x480: the cards of the main screen, key =
      favorites, arcade, computer, console, handheld, other, mister


STEP 2 - OPEN THE FOLDER IN THE EDITOR
  1. Open theme_editor.html and press "Open folder": choose mycitypop and
     allow the editor to change it.
  2. There is no theme.ini yet: the editor says so and starts from a new
     theme with some basic elements. The left panel says how many
     pictures and fonts it found (7 pictures in img/, 7 cards, 3 fonts).

  How the editor works:
  - left: the elements. The one on top is drawn above the others. Click
    an element (in the list or on the screen) to change it on the right;
    "[theme]" holds the settings of the whole screen.
  - "Add" adds an element of the chosen type after the selected one;
    "Delete" removes the selected one; the arrows change the order.
  - right: the keys of the element. Every key of this guide goes into the
    field with the same name; "rect" is x, y, width, height. A key that is
    not in the guide must stay EMPTY: "Add" fills some keys with examples
    (e.g. transition=fade 250 on the pictures, sel_background on the list),
    clear them.
  - a key without a field goes into "Other keys" as key=value.
  - "Undo" (Ctrl+Z) undoes the last change.


STEP 3 - THE LIST, 16:9 (theme.ini)
  1. Select each element of the new theme and press "Delete", until only
     [theme] is left.
  2. Click [theme] and fill in its keys:

  [theme]
    name=City Pop
    base=1280x720
    background=img/bg.png
    background_fit=stretch
    background_color=#F4D5D5
    font=fonts/PTSansNarrow-Bold.ttf
    size=32
    color=#212121

  3. Add the elements in this order, each with "Add" (choose its type,
     the "type" key below, in the menu next to "Add"), then give it the
     name and the keys below. Keep the last added element selected: the
     next one goes after it, so it is drawn above it (and above it in the
     editor list). The order matters where they overlap: the picture above
     its placeholder text, the year above its tag, the letter above its
     background.

  [title]
    type=text
    rect=190,8,834,90
    text=${title}
    font=fonts/BebasNeue-Regular.ttf
    size=72
    color=#69D2BA
    shadow=#3C6BB1
    shadow_offset=4
    scroll=horizontal

  [position]
    type=text
    rect=1030,14,224,84
    text=${pos}
    font=fonts/BebasNeue-Regular.ttf
    size=44
    color=#3C6BB1
    shadow=#7AA1DB
    shadow_offset=3
    align=right

  [list]
    type=list
    rect=42,117,574,510
    row_height=51
    padding=18
    color=#212121
    dir_color=#3C6BB1
    tag_color=#EC76B2
    tag_dir=>
    tag_up=<
    sel_image=img/sel.png
    sel_color=#002967
    sel_scroll=1
    scrollbar_width=4
    scrollbar_color=#EAD9D9
    scrollbar_knob=#FFA3D9
    empty_text=No games

  [no_image]
    type=text
    rect=662,124,566,310
    text=${game.title}
    ifnot=${media.system}${media.snap}${media.title}${media.box}
    font=fonts/BebasNeue-Regular.ttf
    size=60
    color=#E2CACA
    align=center
    wrap=1
    layout=media

  [image]
    type=image
    rect=662,124,566,310
    src=${media.system}|${media.snap}|${media.title}|${media.box}
    fit=contain
    layout=media

  [text_system]
    type=image
    rect=662,124,566,310
    src=${media.system}
    fit=contain
    ifnot=${game.desc}
    layout=text

  [text_desc]
    type=text
    rect=670,180,550,246
    text=${game.desc}
    size=28
    color=#212121
    wrap=1
    valign=top
    scroll=vertical
    layout=text

  [favorite]
    type=text
    rect=662,124,48,48
    text=★
    font=fonts/DejaVuSans-Bold.ttf
    size=40
    color=#FFA3D9
    shadow=#3C6BB1
    shadow_offset=3
    align=center
    if=${game.favorite}

  [year_tag]
    type=image
    rect=1122,96,132,60
    src=img/tag.png
    if=${game.year}

  [year]
    type=text
    rect=1142,96,104,52
    text=${game.year}
    font=fonts/BebasNeue-Regular.ttf
    size=44
    color=#3C6BB1
    align=right

  [letter_shadow]
    type=rect
    rect=896,196,110,110
    color=#7AA1DB
    if=${jump}

  [letter_back]
    type=rect
    rect=890,190,110,110
    color=#69D2BA
    if=${jump}

  [letter]
    type=text
    rect=890,190,110,110
    text=${jump}
    font=fonts/BebasNeue-Regular.ttf
    size=96
    color=#002967
    align=center
    if=${jump}

  [name]
    type=text
    rect=652,452,602,62
    text=${game.title}
    font=fonts/BebasNeue-Regular.ttf
    size=60
    color=#3C6BB1
    shadow=#7AA1DB
    shadow_offset=3
    scroll=horizontal

  [maker]
    type=text
    rect=652,514,602,36
    text=${game.developer}
    color=#585858
    scroll=horizontal

  [genre]
    type=text
    rect=652,550,602,36
    text=${game.genre}
    color=#747474
    scroll=horizontal

  [stars]
    type=text
    rect=652,588,602,40
    text=${game.stars}
    font=fonts/DejaVuSans-Bold.ttf
    size=28
    color=#EC76B2

  [help]
    type=text
    rect=60,648,1160,51
    text=A PLAY    X DETAILS    B BACK    HOLD X FAVORITE
    font=fonts/BebasNeue-Regular.ttf
    size=40
    color=#002967
    ifnot=${message}

  [message]
    type=text
    rect=60,648,1160,51
    text=${message}
    font=fonts/BebasNeue-Regular.ttf
    size=40
    color=#FFFFFF
    shadow=#3C6BB1
    shadow_offset=3
    if=${message}

  What the elements do:
    title, position   name of the folder and position in the list, top
    list              the games; the mint bar is sel_image
    no_image, image   the picture of the game (or of the system, on the
                      main screen); without a picture, the name in pale pink
    text_system, text_desc
                      with Text layout ON (layout=text) in place of the
                      picture: the description of the game (or of the
                      system), else the picture of the theme; no_image and
                      image have layout=media (only with it OFF)
    favorite          star on the picture for the games in the favorites
    year_tag, year    pink tag with the year
    letter_*          the letter chosen with L/R, for a second
    name, maker...    data of the game under the picture
    help, message     help line on the mint bar; the messages ("Added to
                      favorites"...) take its place for a moment

  4. "Save to folder": the editor writes theme.ini in mycitypop.

STEP 7 - ON THE MISTER
  1. Copy the folder mycitypop to /media/fat/frontier/themes/ (network
     share, FTP/SCP or the SD card in the PC).
  2. In Frontier choose Frontier Settings (last entry of the main screen) -> "Theme" and
     press A until the theme is "City Pop" again (the name is in [theme],
     change it to tell them apart), or write theme=mycitypop in /media/fat/frontier/frontier.ini
     and press F5 (keyboard) in Frontier.
  3. To change it later: edit in the editor, "Save to folder", copy the
     changed files, F5.

  Import .ini: loads .ini files into the editor without opening their
  folder. Open mycitypop, then "Import .ini" and choose the files of
  themes/citypop (theme.ini, detail.ini...): you see the finished theme
  with your pictures, and you can compare it with yours. Files named
  theme.ini, theme_43.ini, detail.ini, detail_43.ini go into their
  variant, a file with another name into the current one. The fonts of
  the imported files are ../simple/fonts/...: write fonts/... to see them.


STEP 8 - THE DARK AND BLUE VARIANTS
  City Pop Dark (themes/citypop_dark) and City Pop Blue
  (themes/citypop_blue) have the same elements and other colours: their
  pictures are made with other colours by the same script, and their .ini
  files are those of City Pop with these changes (in all four files):

  City Pop Dark (the dark mode of bubblism.jp)
    pictures: background #212121, grid #553C50, panels #2B2B2B
    [theme] background_color=#212121
    every color=#212121 -> #DCDCDC (text)
    color=#3C6BB1 -> #7AA1DB and shadow=#7AA1DB -> #3C6BB1, except in
      [year] (on the pink tag)
    color=#585858 -> #A8A8A8, color=#747474 -> #8F8F8F
    color=#E2CACA -> #3A3A3A (no_image), scrollbar_color=#3A3A3A

  City Pop Blue (background of the blue of the titles)
    pictures: background #3C6BB1, grid #5682C4, panels #F5F7FF, shadows
      of the panels and edge of the header slab #002967
    [theme] background_color=#3C6BB1
    scrollbar_color=#D8DAE1, no_image color=#D8DAE1
    on the elements on the background (title, position, name, maker, genre,
      players, stars): shadow=#002967, color=#3C6BB1 -> #F5F7FF,
      #585858 -> #DCE6F7, #747474 -> #B4C8EA, #EC76B2 -> #FFA3D9
